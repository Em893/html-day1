INCEPTION

A mind-blowing journey

Inception is my all-time favourite movie. It stars Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb, a thief who steals secrets from people's dreams.

The film is famous for its dream within a dream concept. Cobb must perform "inception" — planting an idea inside someone's mind. It's smart, emotional, and thrilling.

Memorable quote:

"An idea is like a virus. Resilient, highly contagious."

Why I love it:

  • Amazing visual effects and music
  • Twisted storyline that makes you think
  • Powerful ending that everyone debates

Main cast:

  • Leonardo DiCaprio as Cobb
  • Joseph Gordon-Levitt as Arthur
  • Elliot Page as Ariadne
  • Tom Hardy as Eames

Rating: ★★★★★
If you haven't seen Inception, watch it tonight!
